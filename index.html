How It All Began

Our Story

Neither of us knew that a moment would change everything.

What started as a simple hello turned into conversations we did not want to end. Long calls, late nights and laughter. Somewhere along the way we found ourselves dreaming together too — building a business idea from the ground up, learning what the other was made of in the process.

We grew alongside each other. We challenged each other. We showed up, consistently, in the quiet ways that matter most. And the more time we spent together, the harder it became to imagine life without the other in it.

It was not a lightning bolt moment. It was something better — a slow, warm realisation that this person had already become our favourite part of every day.

We did not plan any of this. But we know who did. God placed us in the same room, knit our paths together, and in His quiet, intentional way, wrote a love story neither of us could have written for ourselves.

This was always His plan for us, and we are so grateful it found us.
